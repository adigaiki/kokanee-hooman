Everyday curry

Hooman,
the way home tastes.

A coconut-forward curry made for rice, conversation, and returning to the table.

The story

One word, many tables.

Hooman is a word that carries more than one curry. It is the familiar rhythm of rice, a ladle, and a pot that can stretch to welcome another person at lunch.

Across Goa, the balance changes with the catch, the season, and the cook. But the essential gesture remains: a bright, tangy curry that is never in a hurry to announce itself.

This version is a home-style foundation. Make it with fish, vegetables, or simply what the day gives you.

“A good hooman makes room for one more plate.”

Ingredients

For a shared lunch

  • 2 cups fresh grated coconut
  • 6 dried red chillies
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Small marble-sized tamarind
  • 1 cup water, plus more as needed
  • Salt, to taste
  • 500g fish or seasonal vegetables

Method

Build the balance.

  1. Blend the coconut, chillies, coriander, turmeric, tamarind and water until the paste is smooth.
  2. Bring the paste to a gentle simmer in a wide pot, thinning with water until it has the consistency you like.
  3. Add salt, then add fish or vegetables and cook gently until just done.
  4. Rest the curry for a few minutes before serving with steamed rice.
